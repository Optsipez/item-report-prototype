Weekly sales

Weekly figures are estimated — the source data is monthly totals, spread across days with a weekend weighting. Weeks run Monday–Sunday. The chart re-centres on whichever month cell you clicked, showing it plus the two months before it; that month's own weeks are highlighted in blue. The most recent week is partial only when it's still in progress.

13-month trend

The current month gets its own column, then four 3-month periods going backwards, using all 13 months. Each column is always fully coloured — Stock (gold) fills down from the top, Sold (blue) fills up from the bottom, meeting at Sold's actual share of the two that period. A fixed white line marks the exact centre and never moves; when the blue rises past it, Sold has overtaken Stock. Real totals and the sell-through rate (Sold ÷ (Stock + Sold)) are shown underneath each bar.
